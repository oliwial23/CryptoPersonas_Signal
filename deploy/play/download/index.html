Signal Personas

A research prototype of Cryptographic Personas: a group chat where members can post anonymously or under personas, rate posts, and vote to ban someone — all without anyone, including the server, learning who wrote what. It runs on a private test server, separate from the real Signal.

Download Signal Personas for Mac Apple Silicon (M1 or later) · macOS 12 or later · about 140 MB

1. Install

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Signal Personas into Applications.
  2. Open it from Applications. macOS will refuse the first time, because the app is not registered with Apple:
    macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later: click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Signal Personas, click Open Anyway, and confirm.

    macOS 14 or earlier: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.
    You only need to do this once.
  3. Choose a display name. Other testers see it next to your account. It does not need to be your real name.
  4. Give it a minute: the app creates a test account (with a made-up phone number — no SMS, no real number needed) and downloads the shared cryptographic keys (about 50 MB).

It installs alongside the real Signal without touching it: separate app, separate data, separate server.

2. Things to try

  • Find people. Other testers who have the app open appear in your contacts automatically.
  • Make a group. Create a new group and add two or more people. The app sets up the shared anonymous identity for everyone in it; give it about 30 seconds after creating the group.
  • Post anonymously. Nobody in the group can tell who sent it — not even from the message metadata.
  • Post as a persona. A persona is a pseudonym. Posts under the same persona are linked to each other but not to you, and you can use several personas that nobody can link together.
  • React and rate. Reputation attaches to personas, not to people.
  • Run a ban poll (needs at least three members). A banned persona's author can no longer post — enforced by zero-knowledge proofs, not by trusting the server.

Playing several members yourself

A ban poll needs three members, and some things are easiest to see from both sides. You can run up to two extra members on the same Mac: choose File → Open Extra Member 1 (or 2). Each opens in its own window, asks for its own display name the first time, and appears to everyone else as a separate person. The member's name is shown in each window's title. Choosing an extra member that is already open just brings its window to the front.

You can also ask whoever invited you about the browser members (alice, bob, carol) you can control from a web page.

3. Good to know

This is a test system. Do not share anything sensitive. Accounts and messages may be wiped whenever the test server is reset.
  • Who sees what. Your display name is visible to other testers. What you post anonymously or under a persona is not linked to it. The test server delivers messages but cannot read them or tell who wrote a persona post.
  • Posting at the exact same moment as someone else can occasionally drop one of the two posts. If a post does not show up for others, send it again.
  • Slow first post. Each persona post includes a zero-knowledge proof made on your Mac, which can take a few seconds.
  • If the test server is reset, everyone's account is wiped. The app notices, restarts, and registers again with the same name; your contacts come back within a minute or two, but groups have to be created again.
  • Contacts only list people whose app has been open in the last hour.
  • The app never updates itself; you will be sent a new download when there is a new version.

4. Reporting a problem

  1. Note what you did and roughly when.
  2. In the app, open View → Debug Log and choose Save to keep it as a file. (Uploading the log is turned off in this build, so nothing is sent anywhere.)
  3. Send the file and your notes to the person who invited you.

5. Uninstalling

Quit the app, delete Signal Personas from Applications, and delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Signal-Personas (plus Signal-Personas-extra-1 and -extra-2 if you opened extra members).
