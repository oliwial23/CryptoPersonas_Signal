Signal Personas

A research prototype of Cryptographic Personas: a group chat where you can post anonymously or under pseudonyms, rate posts, earn badges and ban bad actors — without anyone, including the server, learning who wrote what. It runs on a private test server, separate from the real Signal.

Download for Apple Silicon M1, M2, M3, M4 or later · about 150 MB Download for Intel Mac macOS 12 or later · about 155 MB

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac. It says Chip: Apple M… (Apple Silicon) or Processor: … Intel (Intel).

⚠️
Personas work in ONE group per app, for now. The first group you create (or are added to with personas on) becomes your personas group. Any other group — on your main account or on an extra member — will not have personas. So: make one test group, and add everyone, including your extra members, to that group. Do not create a second group.
1

Install

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Signal Personas into Applications.
  2. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS refuses because the app is not registered with Apple:
    • macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later: click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about Signal Personas, click Open Anyway and confirm.
    • macOS 14 or earlier: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again.
    You only need to do this once.
  3. Choose a display name. Other testers see it; it does not have to be your real name.
  4. Wait about a minute while it creates a test account (a made-up number, no SMS) and downloads the shared cryptographic keys.

It installs next to the real Signal without touching it: separate app, data and server.

2

Make your test group

  1. Click New chat (the pencil at the top of the chat list), then New group.
  2. On Choose members, pick any other testers you see — or click Skip. You will add your own second account to this group in step 5.
  3. Give it a name (for example Personas test) and click Create.
  4. Wait about 30 seconds. The app turns personas on for this group automatically and gives it its own anonymous identity.
Joining someone else's group instead? That works too: once they add you, that group becomes your personas group. Just don't also create your own.
Group made before this version? A group admin opens the persona button (step 3) and chooses Turn on personas in this group. Only one admin should do this.

4

Post anonymously

  1. In your test group, click the persona button and choose Anonymous.
  2. Type a message and send it. The first one takes a few seconds — your Mac is making a zero-knowledge proof.
  3. It shows as Anonymous. Nobody in the group can tell it was you, not even from the message metadata.
  4. To go back to normal messages, open the persona button and choose Normal message.
5

See it from the other side: add a second account

This is great — but to see how posting anonymously looks to others in the group, you can add a second account on the same Mac. Each extra account opens in its own window and counts as a separate person.

Each window is framed in its own colour, with its name at the top.

a. Open the second account

  1. In your main (green) window, open your test group, click the persona button and choose Persona status….
  2. At the bottom of the panel, click Open extra member 1.
  3. A new window with an orange frame opens. Choose a display name for it (for example Alex) and wait about a minute for it to set up.
Don't create a group in the extra member's window. It would become that account's only personas group. Add it to your existing group instead:

b. Add it to your test group

  1. Go back to your green main window and open your test group.
  2. Click the group's name at the top of the chat to open the group's settings.
  3. Click Add members, pick the extra member's display name, and confirm.
  4. Keep your main window open. Within about 30 seconds your app sends the new member the group's anonymous identity and current state, automatically (never any old message text).
  5. In the orange window, open the group, click the persona button and choose Persona status…. When Group state says Up to date, it is ready.
Now try it: post anonymously from one window and watch it arrive in the other as Anonymous. Then switch roles. Repeat with Open extra member 2 (purple) for a third person — ban polls need three members.
6

Try everything else

These start from the persona button , or from a message's menu (right-click the message, or hover over it and click ⋯).

Pseudonyms

A made-up name like ~eligible turtle. Posts under one pseudonym are linked to each other but not to you, and nobody can link your different pseudonyms together.

Persona button → New pseudonym, then send.

Contexts & rate-limited personas

A context is a topic. In each one you get at most 3 personas (#1–#3), enforced by the proof itself, so nobody can flood a topic with fake voices.

An admin creates one: persona button → New context…. Then choose the context and #1, #2 or #3.

Rate posts

Give a persona post 👍 or 👎. Reputation belongs to the persona, not the person, and only its owner can see it.

Message menu → React → 👍 / 👎. The author's score updates in Persona status after their next post.

Badges

Faculty, Student or Industry — granted by an admin and shown on your posts. You can prove you hold one without revealing who you are.

Persona button → Request badge…. An admin clicks Approve in the chat. It appears after your next post; then choose it under Badge, or use Prove badge….

Prove authorship

Show that two of your pseudonyms are the same person — your choice, and permanent.

Post under two pseudonyms first, then persona button → Prove authorship….

Ban someone

A banned author can no longer post anonymously or as a persona — and still nobody learns who they are.

Message menu → Request ban poll… (anyone; the group votes) or Ban permanently (admins; takes effect within seconds, no poll).

Good to know & help

  • This is a test system. Don't share anything sensitive; accounts and messages may be wiped when the test server is reset.
  • Posting at the exact same moment as someone else can occasionally lose one of the two posts. If a post doesn't show up for others, send it again.
  • Persona posts stopped getting through? Persona status → Request a reset. An admin approves it and you rejoin as a new member (your reputation, badges and personas start over).
  • Contacts list testers whose app was open in the last hour.
  • Server reset: the app re-registers with the same name on its own; groups have to be made again.
  • Updates don't install themselves — you'll be sent a new download.
Reporting a problem
  1. Note what you did and roughly when.
  2. In the app, choose View → Debug Log, then Save. (Nothing is uploaded.)
  3. Send the file and your notes to the person who invited you.
Uninstalling

Quit the app, delete Signal Personas from Applications, and delete the folder ~/Library/Application Support/Signal-Personas (plus Signal-Personas-extra-1 and -extra-2 if you opened extra members).
